## Overview

A PR opened against `main` arrives on [[channel|delivery.github-webhooks]] and starts [[service|pr-preview]]. A preview built on the developer's Mac (`laptop_preview`) comes from the [[service|delivery-cli]] through the [[channel|delivery.harness-pipeline-api]]. The preview is accepted or rejected within 24h, then torn down.
